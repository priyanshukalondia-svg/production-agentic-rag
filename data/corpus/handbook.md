# Returns Policy
Customers may return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided it is in original packaging. Returns after 30 days are not accepted.

# Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping is delivered within
1-2 business days for an additional fee. We ship to over 40 countries.

# Warranty
All electronics carry a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover accidental, liquid, or cosmetic damage.

# Refunds
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days
of the returned item being received and inspected.

# Account Security
Accounts are protected with two-factor authentication. Passwords must be at least
12 characters. Support will never ask for your password by email or phone.
